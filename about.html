About Tresa

Born From a Belief
in Beauty

Tresa was founded in 2009 by a collective of artisans and collectors who believed the modern world had sacrificed meaning for mass production. We set out to create objects that would outlast trends — pieces designed not for the season but for the decade, the generation, the lifetime. Every Tresa creation begins not with a brief, but with a question: what would endure?

From our atelier in Geneva, we work with a small circle of the world's finest craftspeople — ceramicists from Vallauris, silversmiths from Sheffield, weavers from Oaxaca. The result is a table collection that carries the fingerprints of true mastery. We do not scale what cannot be done by hand. We do not sell what we would not keep ourselves. This is the Tresa promise, and it has remained unchanged since the day we opened our doors.

What We Stand For

Craftsmanship

Every piece is made by hand using centuries-old techniques, refined and elevated for the discerning modern table. We believe in the integrity of process as much as outcome.

Integrity

We are transparent about provenance, material sourcing, and pricing. We charge what excellence costs and never less — because authenticity is not negotiable, and neither is trust.

Legacy

We design for inheritance. A Tresa piece is not a purchase but an acquisition — something to be passed down with a story attached. We measure our success in generations, not quarters.
